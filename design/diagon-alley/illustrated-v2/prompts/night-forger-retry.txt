A refined volumetric editorial gouache and oil painting of Rubeus Hagrid, the enormous gentle half-giant from the Harry Potter books. Chest-up three-quarter portrait of a huge broad-shouldered man whose sheer width and massive warm rounded face immediately identify him as a half-giant. Very broad rounded nose, tiny warm black beetle-like eyes beneath heavy bushy eyebrows, rosy weathered cheeks and a kindly gentle smile mostly hidden in a tremendously long thick tangled dark-brown beard that flows all the way down across his entire chest. His beard is exceptionally voluminous, wider than his face and waist-length, with visibly painted individual curls and strands. Long unruly dark-brown hair in thick tangled waves reaching past both shoulders, with complete hair fitting inside the image. His heavy well-worn brown moleskin coat has thick stitched seams, large brass buttons and huge folded collar, no scarf covering the long beard. Natural mature human facial anatomy, subtly painted amber window light on hair and cheeks, deeply layered indigo shadows, realistic coarse leather and wool texture, fine painterly strokes and convincing physical weight. Warm sophisticated illustrated storybook style matching a beautifully detailed magical alley at dusk. Plain unmarked dark muted plum backdrop extends to every edge, full-bleed painting, hands completely below the lower crop, complete head with comfortable margin, no frame or writing.
